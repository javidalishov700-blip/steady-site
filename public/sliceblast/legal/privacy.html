Slice & Blast

Privacy Policy

Slice & Blast · Developer: Javid Alishov · Last updated 23 August 2026

Slice & Blast collects, stores, transmits and shares nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK in the app.

The short version. The game runs entirely on your device. Nothing you do in it leaves your phone, because there is no server for it to leave to.

What we do not collect

None of the following is collected, stored, processed or transmitted, at any point:

  • Name, e-mail address, phone number, or any other contact detail
  • Account credentials — the game has no sign-in of any kind
  • Location, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry or performance traces
  • Purchase history — the app contains no purchases of any kind

The app requests no runtime permissions, and includes no advertising, analytics or attribution framework to request them on its behalf.

What stays on your device

Three small settings are saved locally, using Apple's standard app-preferences storage:

  • Your best score
  • Whether sound is on or off
  • Whether vibration is on or off

This never leaves your device, is not readable by us or anyone else, and is deleted the moment you delete the app. If your device backs up to iCloud, these preferences may ride along inside your own encrypted backup — that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and remains entirely under your control.

Network access

The game makes no network requests during play. The only way it can open a connection at all is if you deliberately tap the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use link on the title screen, which hands the address to your browser — the browser makes that request, not the game.

Children

Slice & Blast is suitable for all ages, and built so that it can be: since it collects nothing at all, there is nothing to collect from a child either. We do not knowingly gather personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose personal data, because we hold none. No third-party advertising, analytics, social or attribution service is embedded in the app.

Apple distributes the app and may collect its own information under Apple's Privacy Policy. That processing belongs to Apple; none of it reaches us.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA grant rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to produce or erase. To clear the local settings above, delete the app.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change with it. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Slice & Blast

Privacy Policy

Slice & Blast · Developer: Javid Alishov · Last updated 28 August 2026

Slice & Blast itself collects, stores and transmits nothing about you — there is still no account, no sign-in and no analytics of our own. The one exception is advertising: the app shows ads served by Google AdMob, and AdMob collects a limited set of identifiers and usage data to do that. That collection, and your controls over it, are described below.

The short version. The game itself runs entirely on your device and keeps no server. Google AdMob, which serves the ads you see, collects device and usage data under its own privacy policy — see "Advertising" below for exactly what and why.

What we do not collect

None of the following is collected, stored, processed or transmitted by us, at any point:

  • Name, e-mail address, phone number, or any other contact detail
  • Account credentials — the game has no sign-in of any kind
  • Location, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data
  • Purchase history — the app contains no in-app purchases of any kind
  • Any analytics or crash telemetry of our own

The app requests no runtime permissions of its own, and includes no analytics or attribution framework. The only third-party code in the app is the Google Mobile Ads SDK, covered next.

Advertising

Slice & Blast shows ads (interstitial and rewarded video) served by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS) and other device identifiers
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed or tapped
  • General device and app information (device model, OS version, IP-derived approximate region, language) used for ad delivery and fraud prevention

On iOS, the first time this matters the system shows Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether Slice & Blast may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track (or have that turned on system-wide), AdMob still serves ads, just less targeted to you — declining does not remove the ads or affect anything else in the game.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never see it. Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and its page on how partner sites and apps use data (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) cover what Google does with it and the controls Google gives you — including Ad Settings to see and adjust ad personalisation, and your device's own Settings to reset or limit its advertising identifier.

What stays on your device

Three small settings are saved locally, using Apple's standard app-preferences storage:

  • Your best score
  • Whether sound is on or off
  • Whether vibration is on or off

This never leaves your device, is not readable by us or anyone else, and is deleted the moment you delete the app. If your device backs up to iCloud, these preferences may ride along inside your own encrypted backup — that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and remains entirely under your control.

Network access

Outside of loading an ad, the game makes no network requests during play. The only other way it can open a connection is if you deliberately tap the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use link on the title screen, which hands the address to your browser — the browser makes that request, not the game.

Children

Slice & Blast is not directed at children and is not listed in a children's category. We do not knowingly gather personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and AdMob is configured accordingly. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at the address below.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose personal data ourselves, because we hold none. The only third party embedded in the app is Google AdMob, covered above under "Advertising" — no analytics, social or attribution service is included.

Apple distributes the app and may collect its own information under Apple's Privacy Policy. That processing belongs to Apple; none of it reaches us.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA grant rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data a company holds about you. We hold none ourselves, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase — to clear the local settings above, delete the app. For data AdMob collects on Google's side, use Google Ad Settings or Google's own privacy controls, and your device's Settings to reset or limit its advertising identifier.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change with it. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

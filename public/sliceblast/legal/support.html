Support

Slice & Blast · Developer: Javid Alishov

Slice & Blast is a free one-thumb stacking game. Tap to drop each block — land it dead centre and keep your full width, miss and the overhang is sliced away.

Fastest way to reach us. Email javidalishov700@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version, and it will be looked at.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to drop the moving block.
  • Perfect placements build a streak — three in a row detonates the top of the tower and hands the width back.
  • Special blocks appear rarely and are worth chasing:
    • Neon — land it perfectly and it takes the three layers under it with it.
    • Electric — sends a current up the tower and doubles your score for 15 seconds.
    • Glass — grants a shield that absorbs one mistake whole.
    • Steel — heavy and fast, and it widens the tower back out.
  • Miss a special and it's simply lost — the tower is never damaged for it.

Does the game need an internet connection?

The game itself doesn't — every run plays fully offline. A connection is only used to load the occasional ad (see below); without one, ads simply don't load and play continues normally.

Where is my best score kept?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Are there ads or purchases?

No purchases — the game is free and complete as it is. It does show ads served by Google AdMob: a short one between runs, and an optional one on the run-over screen if you'd rather watch it for a bigger continue block than restart. See the Privacy Policy for what AdMob collects to do that.

Sound or vibration is bothering me

Both can be switched off from the title screen, or from the pause menu mid-run.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 13.0 or later. Portrait only.
